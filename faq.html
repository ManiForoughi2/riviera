FAQ FAQ

What guys usually ask before rush.

Do I need to know anyone?

No. Plenty of guys walk in knowing nobody. Come to an open night and talk to whoever's there.

When is rush?

Every fall and spring. The schedule changes each semester, so fill out the rush form and we'll send you the dates.

Where is GLV?

The Greek Leadership Village on ASU's Tempe campus. Our house is the one with the ΦΓΔ letters out front.

What should I wear?

For open nights, whatever you'd wear to class. We'll tell you ahead of time if a night needs more than that.

How much does it cost?

Dues change from year to year, so ask the rush chairs for this semester's number. They'll give you a straight answer.

Who do I talk to?

Lincoln Sneed and Carson Ziegler are the rush chairs. DM @asufiji and it gets to them.
