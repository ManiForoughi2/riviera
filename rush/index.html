Questions

What is FIJI?

FIJI is the nickname of Phi Gamma Delta, founded in 1848. Our chapter has been at Arizona State since 1965.

Do I need to know anyone?

No. Plenty of guys walk in knowing nobody. Come to an open night and talk to whoever's there.

When is rush?

Every fall and spring. Spring '27 dates are still being set. Get on the list or follow @asufiji to hear them first.

Where is GLV?

The Greek Leadership Village on ASU's Tempe campus, near Rural Road and Terrace Road.

What should I wear?

For open nights, whatever you'd wear to class. We'll tell you ahead of time if a night needs more than that.

How much does it cost?

Dues change from year to year, so ask the rush chairs for this semester's number. They'll give you a straight answer.

Who do I talk to?

Matthew Trapp and Nolan Walters are the rush chairs. DM @asufiji and it gets to them.
